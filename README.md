# parking-violations-analysis-in-NYC

In this data analysis project, I analyze over 4 million traffic violations committed in New York City's Metropolitan area to understand which traffic violations are most common, by who, and where.  Most Traffic violations occur in Manhattan but are overall evenly distributed amongst Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Queens
